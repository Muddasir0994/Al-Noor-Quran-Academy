Essential Daily Masnoon Supplications from Hisnul Muslim
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ
1. Before Starting Any Action
Bismillahir Rahmanir Raheem
In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful.
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الَّذِي لَا يَضُرُّ مَعَ اسْمِهِ شَيْءٌ فِي الْأَرْضِ وَلَا فِي السَّمَاءِ وَهُوَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ
2. Morning & Evening Protection Dua
Bismillahilladhi la yadurru ma'as-mihi shay'un fil-ardi wa la fis-sama'i wa huwas-Samee'ul-'Aleem.
In the name of Allah, with Whose name nothing can cause harm in the earth nor in the heavens, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing. (Recite 3x morning & evening).
رَبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا
3. Dua for Increase in Knowledge (Before Quran Class)
Rabbi Zidnee 'Ilmaa
O my Lord! Increase me in knowledge. (Surah Ta-Ha 20:114)
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ عِلْمًا نَافِعًا وَرِزْقًا طَيِّبًا وَعَمَلًا مُتَقَبَّلًا
4. Morning Dua for Beneficial Knowledge & Halal Rizq
Allahumma inni as'aluka 'ilman nafi'an, wa rizqan tayyiban, wa 'amalan mutaqabbala.
O Allah! I ask You for knowledge that is of benefit, a good provision, and deeds that are accepted.

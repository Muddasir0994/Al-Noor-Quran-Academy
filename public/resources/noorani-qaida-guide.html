Complete 29 Arabic Alphabets & Makharij Articulation Guide
ا
Alif (أَلِف)
Empty space of mouth (Jawf)
ب
Baa (بَاء)
Wet inner part of both lips
ت
Taa (تَاء)
Tip of tongue + upper incisor roots
ث
Thaa (ثَاء)
Tip of tongue + edge of upper teeth
ج
Jeem (جِيم)
Middle of tongue + hard palate
ح
Haa (حَاء)
Middle of the throat (Wasat-ul-Halq)
خ
Khaa (خَاء)
Top of the throat near mouth
د
Daal (دَال)
Tip of tongue + upper incisors
ذ
Dhaal (ذَال)
Tip of tongue + upper teeth edges
ر
Raa (رَاء)
Edge of tongue tip + upper gums
ز
Zaa (زَاي)
Tip of tongue + between teeth
س
Seen (سِين)
Tip of tongue with whistling sound
ش
Sheen (شِين)
Middle of tongue + upper palate
ص
Saad (صَاد)
Heavy whistling letter from tongue tip
ض
Daad (ضَاد)
Side edge of tongue + upper molars
ط
Taa (طَاء)
Heavy emphatic T from tongue tip
ظ
Zaa (ظَاء)
Heavy emphatic Dh from teeth edge
ع
Ayn (عَيْن)
Middle of the throat (Clear)
غ
Ghayn (غَيْن)
Upper throat near uvula
ف
Faa (فَاء)
Bottom lip + top incisors
ق
Qaaf (قَاف)
Deep back of tongue near uvula (Heavy)
ك
Kaaf (كَاف)
Back of tongue slightly forward (Light)
ل
Laam (لاَم)
Side of tongue tip + upper gums
م
Meem (مِيم)
Dry outer surface of both lips
ن
Noon (نُون)
Tip of tongue + upper gums + nasal Ghunnah
و
Waaw (وَاو)
Rounding of both lips
ه
Haa (هَاء)
Deepest part of throat near chest
ء
Hamzah (هَمْزَة)
Deepest part of throat (glottal stop)
ي
Yaa (يَاء)
Middle of tongue + upper palate
